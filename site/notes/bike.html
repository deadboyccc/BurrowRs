Reviving a 1980s steel road bike

Workshop

It came from a neighbour's shed with flat tyres and a seat post that had not moved since the last century.

The stuck seat post

Penetrating oil every evening for a week, then a lot of patience. Ammonia helps with aluminium posts in steel frames, but go slowly.

Cottered cranks

Support the crank arm properly before you press the cotter out; a hammer alone will bend the spindle. Replacement cotters need filing to fit.

Total cost: two tyres, cables, bar tape and far too many evenings.
