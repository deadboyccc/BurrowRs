A lazier sourdough schedule

Kitchen

Most recipes assume you are home all day. I am not, so the dough does its slow work in the fridge instead.

The schedule

  1. Evening: mix 500 g flour, 360 g water, 100 g starter, 10 g salt.
  2. Two sets of folds 30 minutes apart, then into the fridge, covered.
  3. Morning: pre-shape, rest 20 minutes, shape, back into the fridge.
  4. Evening: bake straight from cold at 250 °C, lid on for 20 minutes, off for 25.

The long cold proof gives more sourness and a blistered crust. If your fridge runs warm, cut the starter to 70 g.
