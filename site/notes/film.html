Developing black-and-white film at home

Photography

You don't need a darkroom, just a changing bag and a light-tight tank. Everything after loading the reel happens with the lights on.

What I use

  • Steel tank and reels (plastic works, steel is easier to clean)
  • A one-shot developer at 1+25, 20 °C
  • Water stop bath, rapid fixer, a drop of wetting agent

Lessons from the ruined roll

Check the temperature twice. Four degrees too warm and the negatives came out dense enough to read a newspaper through, barely.
